# Number recap (Y9 Foundation) — plan

Status: implemented (build script `number-recap-y9.build.ts`, deck `number-recap-y9`).

One whiteboard lesson before the Year 9 Number 1 Foundation assessment (14 questions, 38 marks). It revises the skills the test needs through fresh numbers and contexts, not test-style questions. Blocks began in order of the marks they carry; Harry then moved the two quick 2-mark blocks (order of operations, negatives) earlier and "closest to a fraction" to the end, so the deck runs: decimals, scaling, multiplying, order of operations, negatives, estimating, money, prime factors, HCF, closest to a fraction, Friday's list. Anything cut for time is cut from the end.

How each block runs: question 1 is cold (no help, tally the boards: this tally chooses the next lesson's starter), then the fix, then a confirmer; the third question is a spare. All on mini-whiteboards.

| Order | Block | Test marks | Questions (all fixed, answers recomputed in the build script) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decimals, scaling, multiplying, closest to a fraction | 11 | 6.84 + 3.57; 8.2 − 3.45; 9.06 and 4.8; 4.05 × 100; 7.3 ÷ 1000; 0.63 × 1000; 67 × 43; 54 × 28; 83 × 36; closest to 1/4, 1/5, 3/4 |
| 2 | Estimating and money | 7 | (28.7 × 9.8) ÷ 0.52; (6.1 × 19.7) ÷ 0.39; (82 × 4.9) ÷ 0.21; plan X v plan Y (150 texts); tiered 50-minute call; taxi A v B |
| 3 | Prime factors to HCF (no Venn) | 4 | 90, 140, 252 as prime products; HCF of 126 and 84 (both given); 150 given, 210 to find; 132 and 198 |
| 4 | Order of operations | 2 | 2 + 3 × 4 − 1; add one pair of brackets to make 19; Dev's 6 × (10 − 4) error |
| 5 | Negatives and squares | 2 | order −9, 4, −2, −15, 0; (−6)² and −6²; Zoe's (−5)² = −25 error |

Traps: the distance from the target (not biggest or smallest) in "closest to"; placeholder zero in 8.2 − 3.45; the digits move, not the point; round every number to 1 s.f. before dividing, and ÷ 0.5 doubles; cost every plan in one unit before comparing; the bracket comes first even when the multiplication is written before it; (−6)² against −6².

Deliberately left out: cube-root equation, a/b HCF-LCM question and error intervals (the test's last three questions, 12 marks).
