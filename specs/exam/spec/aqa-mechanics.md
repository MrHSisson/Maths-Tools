# AQA Further Maths — Optional application 1: Mechanics (spec content)

Source: AQA A-level Further Mathematics 7367 (v1.1, exams June 2019 onwards), section 3.3 "Optional application 1 – mechanics" (MA–ME), notation table 6.12 and Appendix B (Mechanics formulae), extracted from the specification PDF. Statements are quoted as printed; mathematical symbols the PDF text layer garbled (ω, λ, μ, ∫, fractions) were restored by hand from the surrounding wording. The AS paper (7366) was not checked, so there is no AS column.

**Assessment.** Mechanics is one of three optional applications (Mechanics MA–ME, Statistics SA–SH, Discrete DA–DG); students study two. Paper 3 is two 1-hour booklets (100 marks in all, 2 hours, 33⅓% of the A-level) in one of three pairings: Statistics + Mechanics, Mechanics + Discrete, or Discrete + Statistics. Mechanics papers are coded `7367/3M`.

**Assumed from the compulsory content / A-level Maths:** radians (stated in MD1), integration and differentiation, vectors, constant-acceleration (suvat) motion, resolving forces, Newton's second law, friction.

## Formulae students must know (Appendix B, Mechanics)

Not given in the exam; may be provided only as the starting point of a proof or as a result to be proved.

- Forces and equilibrium: weight = mass × g; friction F ≤ μR; Newton's second law F = ma.
- Kinematics, straight-line motion with variable acceleration: v = dr/dt, a = dv/dt = d²r/dt², r = ∫ v dt, v = ∫ a dt.
- Also in the Pure appendix and used by mechanics: volumes of revolution V = π∫y² dx (about the x-axis), scalar product, simple harmonic motion ẍ = −ω²x (listed with the compulsory formulae, not a mechanics statement).
