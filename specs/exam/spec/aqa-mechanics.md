# AQA Further Maths — Optional application 1: Mechanics (spec content)

Source: AQA A-level Further Mathematics 7367 (v1.1, exams June 2019 onwards), section 3.3 "Optional application 1 – mechanics" (MA–ME), notation table 6.12 and Appendix B (Mechanics formulae), extracted from the specification PDF. Statements are quoted as printed; mathematical symbols the PDF text layer garbled (ω, λ, μ, ∫, fractions) were restored by hand from the surrounding wording. The AS column is from AQA AS Further Mathematics 7366 (v1.2, exams June 2018 onwards), section 3.3, same codes. Y = in the AS spec (with the restriction shown); N = A-level (A2) only.

**Assessment.** Mechanics is one of three optional applications (Mechanics MA–ME, Statistics SA–SH, Discrete DA–DG); students study two. Paper 3 is two 1-hour booklets (100 marks in all, 2 hours, 33⅓% of the A-level) in one of three pairings: Statistics + Mechanics, Mechanics + Discrete, or Discrete + Statistics. Mechanics papers are coded `7367/3M`.

**Assumed from the compulsory content / A-level Maths:** radians (stated in MD1), integration and differentiation, vectors, constant-acceleration (suvat) motion, resolving forces, Newton's second law, friction.

## AS or A2? (Year 1 / Year 2 split)

Same MA–ME codes in both specs. **In AS (7366), 16 of 25 statements; A2 adds 9**, and A2 sharpens five (MB1–MB3, MC1, MC7):

- **AS content:** all of MA (2), MB (4), MC (7), and MD1–MD3.
- **A2 only (9):** MD4 (vectors in circular motion), MD5 (conical pendulum), MD6 (vertical circles), and all of ME (centres of mass, sliding / toppling, equilibrium with moments and couples).
- **Same statement, harder at A2 (resolving):** MB1–MB3 are "resolving will not be required" at AS and "problems which require resolving" at A2. MC7 Power is likewise no resolving at AS. MC1 stops at force along the line of motion at AS and adds WD = Fd cos θ at A2.
- AS papers are 1h30 / 80 marks (Paper 2, two booklets); A-level Paper 3 is 2h / 100 marks.

So the AS/A2 line falls inside chapters MB, MC and MD, not just between them. ME is wholly A2.

## Formulae students must know (Appendix B, Mechanics)

Not given in the exam; may be provided only as the starting point of a proof or as a result to be proved.

- Forces and equilibrium: weight = mass × g; friction F ≤ μR; Newton's second law F = ma.
- Kinematics, straight-line motion with variable acceleration: v = dr/dt, a = dv/dt = d²r/dt², r = ∫ v dt, v = ∫ a dt.
- Also in the Pure appendix and used by mechanics: volumes of revolution V = π∫y² dx (about the x-axis), scalar product, simple harmonic motion ẍ = −ω²x (listed with the compulsory formulae, not a mechanics statement).
