## 2026-11-21 — Further Mechanics: flashcards, the mechanics diagram system, and the Work Done and Energy tools

Branch `claude/laughing-wright-lanp0q`. Area: `src/shared/mechanics/`, `src/shared/flashcards/`, `src/tools/FurtherMechanics/`, `src/shared/components/SpecLanding.tsx`.

- **Flashcards (live in the Further Mechanics hub, under Revision).** A shared study screen (`src/shared/flashcards/`) for any Further Maths topic. A student picks a topic (statements, AS or AS and A2) and then a deck: Formulas, Definitions, Implications or Common mistakes (or a mix); each deck shows its size and a coloured bar of their three piles (learning, getting there, secure). The card turns over; the student says Not yet or Got it; Not yet sends a card back to the first pile, Got it moves it up, a secure card returns after a week. Every front is a clear prompt; formula and definition cards can also be asked back to front; some cards carry a mechanics diagram. The piles are kept on the device only. The first set is Work, Energy and Power (MC1 to MC7), in our own words (`src/flashcards/mechanicsMC.ts`). Revised twice on the day after Harry's reviews: the first version's essential/derived split and kinds (unit, method, property…) became the four groups, and the fronts were reworded. Tests check every card's KaTeX, ids, groups and wording rules, the schedule and the stored memory. Route `/further-maths-flashcards`. Plan: `specs/flashcards/further-mechanics-mc.md`. Printing the cards is not built.

- **Work Done (MC1, MC5) built, not yet live** (`/work-done`, `enabled: false`, Developing-tools mode). The first of the four Work, Energy and Power tools, on `DecisionShell` with a mechanics diagram and the written working as its picture. Five sub-tools, each a single skill: **Force and Distance**, **Work and Gravity** (vertical, and along a slope at A2), **Force at an Angle (A2)**, **Several Forces** (two forces, a journey with a height change, find a distance first, two stages) and **Variable Force** (polynomial, kx^n, square root, trigonometric, reciprocal; a constant or a limit; a driving force against a resistance that varies, then the speed). Options: Course (AS or A2), Diagram (with the question, built up in the working, or none), Context, Ask for, Units Given, Answer Format, g (9.8 or 10). Each question's prose, picture, working and answer come from one builder, so they cannot disagree; the maths is in `src/shared/mechanics/core.ts` and the context library in `contexts.ts`. `src/tests/workDone.test.ts` reproduces every acceptance example of the brief and re-derives every generated answer by a different route (including a numerical integral for the variable-force questions), checks every KaTeX line and every scene, the Course setting and a 30-question worksheet at each level. The hub pairs MC1 and MC5's greyed buttons with these sub-tools. Still to do: slope settings in Several Forces (S97), the cubic stopping-point question (S69) and a stopping-distance ask; real phone and printer check.
