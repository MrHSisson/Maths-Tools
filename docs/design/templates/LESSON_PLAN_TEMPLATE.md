**Lesson:** <topic> · **Class / year:** · **Length:** <minutes> · **Tool it uses (if any):** <tool>

**Objective:** what pupils can do by the end.

**Starting point:** what they should already know, and anything they usually get wrong.

**Class profile:** the set and attainment, the calculator (which model), how much writing they can do in a lesson, and the pace you want (for example "one lesson; no book set longer than nine questions"). The numbers, the amount of practice and the wording are sized to this, so a first draft is not too long, too easy or too full of jargon.
