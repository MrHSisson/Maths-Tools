- **Status:** rolling out
- **Measure:** workedSplit

## Intention
Every Worked Example shows step-by-step working that ends on the answer, with a picture that develops beside it where one fits.

## Progress
- Step-by-step reveal with working-step fragments (one written mark per press) is live for every ToolShell tool.
- Developing picture (`stepVisualRenderer`, three layouts) on the tools shown by Matrix → Developing picture.
- Graph builds (SmartGrapher), diagram tools with per-step emphasis (Angles in Triangles is the reference), a single answer step where the last step states the answer (`hideAnswerStep`), rewrite-the-line working (Order of Operations) and the gradient triangle (Properties of Line Equations).

## Next steps

- More pictures: Iteration and Completing the Square graph builds, a Rounding digit-rule picture, Recipes on a ratio table, fractions and percentages on a bar model, Estimation and fractions on a number line.
- Sandbox viewports inside the steps, and picture composites (a step carrying two pictures).
